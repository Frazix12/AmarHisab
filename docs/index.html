Hi, I'm the Founder 👋

I built Amar Hisab because I needed a way to track my grocery spending without the complexity of full accounting software.

I wanted something offline-first so I wasn't dependent on a server, and flexible enough to handle both quick grocery lists and serious expense tracking.

Hopefully you feel that care and attention to detail when you use the app to manage your daily life :)
